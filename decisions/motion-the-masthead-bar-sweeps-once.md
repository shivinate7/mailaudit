# The masthead bar turns green a beat late

The overall progress bar's green fill lands a beat after its width, so the two read as cause and effect. Reaching 100 percent here is rare, and it is the largest thing the app has to say.

## The argument, as recorded

  so the two read as cause and effect rather than one event, and sweeps once.
  It is reachable but rare: 100% there means every card in the ledger has
  arrived, which is the largest thing this app has to say.
