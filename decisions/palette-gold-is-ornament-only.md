# Gold is ornament only, never text

The `gold` token is ornament only. It carries no meaning. At 2.09:1 on `card`, it fails the contrast floor for text by a wide margin. It has two uses, both ornament: the masthead rules, and the sheen across a completed package. The measured ratio is in the `palette-contrast` record.

**Holds while:** `gold` is `#C9A961` in `shared/tokens.json`, and no text uses it. Its two uses are `background` values in the `<style>` block: `.mdl-rule i` and `.mdl-gild`.
