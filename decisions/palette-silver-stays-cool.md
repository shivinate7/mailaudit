# Silver must stay a cool grey

The `silver` token was chosen on looks, not on legibility. The `line` colour it replaced measured better against the violet fill: 3.58:1, against 3.17:1 for `silver`. Both clear the 3:1 floor for graphical objects, so the trade is fair. If `silver` is ever revisited, it must stay **cool**. Every warm metal tested (pewter, deep parchment) fell below 3:1 against violet. The measured ratios are in `palette-contrast`.
