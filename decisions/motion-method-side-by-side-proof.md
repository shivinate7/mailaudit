# Each motion was chosen by looking, not by reading

Every moment in the motion design was built two or three ways, as a side-by-side proof sheet. Every variable but one was held constant. The winning version was picked by looking at it. It was not picked by reading about it.

**Holds while:** `motion-timings` holds the timings that these sheets chose.

`_studies.html` is not in the repo, but the method is worth keeping.
