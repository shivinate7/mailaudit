# The masthead is one letterhead, not stacked boxes

The top of the page reads as one composed letterhead. It holds a wax seal, two gold rules, the title, the house line, and the tallies under a hairline. The stats used to sit in their own bordered card, and the two edges fought each other. The ruled-head-heights record has the measured gap.
