# Claim 9 can pass a box just under 44px

Claim 9 reads fractional box edges up to 1px high. A 43.5px box therefore reads as 44px, so claim 9 can pass a box just under 44px. The gap closes when claim 9 reads the unrounded edges and fails such a box.
