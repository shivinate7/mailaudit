# The lost-photo clock runs from envelope age

**What.** The 7-day lost-photo clock runs from the envelope's `createdAt`. A phone that is offline for more than 7 days with an unsent photo shows a false "Lost" on the other device. The warning clears when the phone uploads the photo.

**Status:** open. Accepted by the owner's 2026-10-17 choice.

**Decision:** `pull-warns-on-what-it-drops`.
