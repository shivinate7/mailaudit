Central idea

Each seller is an account that owes you cards; mail day is reconciling the book until every balance reads zero.

A mail-day session

  1. Open the phone flat. The book lies open, oldest balance on top.
  2. Read the sender on an envelope. Tap that account. Its invoice unfolds in place.
  3. Receive each line with + on the right page. Bought 4, got 3 leaves 1.12 due.
  4. A settled account is ruled off with a double gold line.
  5. A blank envelope goes to Suspense. Name its cards, then post it to the account it fits.
  6. An old balance carries Write off, as lost or refunded.

Better than a phone list

Every row shows what you paid and what is still due on one line. The money question has a standing answer. Aging is a column, not a filter.

Suspense is a real ledger idea, so a no-name envelope is normal, not an error.

Biggest risk

It is a table. At hundreds of rows it may feel like accounting, not mail. The rows must cross the fold cleanly, or the gutter breaks the reading line.
