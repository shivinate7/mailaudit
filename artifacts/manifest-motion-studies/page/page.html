C.H Postal Company · Plates I–VI

Six moments in Manifest that had no motion at all, each drawn two or three ways with every variable but one held constant. The variant marked shipped is what is in the app now — the others are still here so you can overrule me. Tap a specimen to replay it.

Plate I · the variable is easing

The RECEIVED stamp lands

A package completes and the green stamp replaces the outstanding pill. It is already rotated −4°; today it simply blinks into being. A stamp should arrive from above and stop.

Plate II · the variable is weight

The wax seal, on first load

The masthead's centre ornament, drawn as a five-value relief ramp so it reads as pressed rather than printed. Once per load, never on re-render.

II c moves nothing at all — worth comparing honestly, since the masthead is what the page opens on and a bouncing seal would be the first thing anyone sees.
Plate III · the variable is the tail

The gold rules draw

Both hairlines grow outward from the seal, so the masthead assembles around its centre rather than fading in as a block.

Plate IV · the variable is where the moment is

A package completes

This was going to be the progress bar — accent violet turning green at 100%, unmarked today. Then the measurement: the bar is mounted only while gotQty > 0 && !done, so it is gone before it can ever render full. The bar cannot mark its own completion because it does not survive it. The card does.

The gold is the one token in the palette that is never allowed to carry information (2.2:1 on card). Here it carries none — it says only that something finished.
Plate V · the variable is the glyph

A card is checked in

The one moment that happens hundreds of times on a mail day, so it has the least room for indulgence. The box is a fixed 30 px and nothing here changes its size — only what happens inside it.

Invariant 5 watch: none of these move the row, change the box's 30 px, or touch anything a finger is about to land on.
Plate VI · the variable is duration

The view switch

Three equal thirds, and the violet fill currently teleports between them. Sliding it makes the switch one object being moved rather than three lamps being lit. Tap a segment.
