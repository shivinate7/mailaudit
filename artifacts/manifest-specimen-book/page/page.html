iOS bakes apple-mobile-web-app-title in at install time. It still reads "Mail Day" on purpose — changing it forces a delete-and-re-add of the home-screen icon, so the frozen label and the presented MANIFEST title are allowed to disagree.

The seal

An envelope's flap, stroked twice — a deep cut line and a raised highlight offset 3.5px, so the fold reads as pressed rather than printed. The outline is a hand-built path, not a circle: real wax is never a circle. One radial gradient carries the relief from waxLit through wax to waxDeep; a single tilted ellipse is the specular catch.

Two copies, one rule set

The masthead's seal presses in on load (mdl-press, 600ms) and its highlight comes up after (mdl-shine). The running head above carries a second, un-animated copy — its shine stays fixed at the same opacity:.22 the first settles to, which is also exactly what reduced motion falls back to. One keyframe set, two resting states, by construction rather than a second stylesheet.

Palette D▸ — warm parchment, violet ink and accent, gold secondary. Nineteen values, and every one that carries information is contrast-checked against card, the surface most text actually sits on. No pure white anywhere in the theme.

The Arial leak

Before — UA default
After — inherited

Browsers force their own UI font onto button/input/select/textarea — setting the family on the root isn't enough. One rule fixed every control at once:

button, input, select, textarea { font-family: inherit; }

jsdom has no UA stylesheet doing this, so no test could ever catch the regression — it was found by reading getComputedStyle in a real browser, and that's the only way it will be found again.

The ruled head's grammar — hairlines, uppercase mono micro-labels over Cochin values, no boxes, no fill but the accent. It replaced a tray of buttons sharing a control height and nothing else.

View switch

One object moving, not three lamps lighting. The thumb is an absolutely positioned fill translated by whole multiples of its own width — it can't drift from the thirds the grid already defines. Click to move it.

Order stamp band

Pressed Flora Cards
○ untracked · order #71029
2 left · refunded
REFUNDED · 09-05 seller confirmed via PayPal, case #4471 ›

Six kinds, chosen by hand: Claim filed, Refunded, Seller contacted, Reshipped, Partial refund, Other. Picking another kind replaces the stamp and re-dates it; editing only the note keeps the date. A refunded or partial package leaves every count the way a canceled order does — the money is back, so nothing is outstanding.

The register is stationery, not software: ink settling, a seal pressed, a stamp rocked onto the page, rules drawn like a pen stroke, gold catching light once. Nothing springs, nothing bounces past its rest and hangs there.

Reduced motion

Every rule above is switched off under prefers-reduced-motion, and the resting state is each animation's own final frame — animation:none leaves the thing drawn, not hidden. Toggle the box: the tiles above should stop mid-gesture at their settled pose, never blank.

A ledger of the passes themselves — dated, in the History panel's own idiom. Method note first: each moment here was built two or three ways as a side-by-side proof sheet with every variable but one held constant, and picked by looking rather than by reading a description.
