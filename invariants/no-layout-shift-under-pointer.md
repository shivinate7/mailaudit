# No layout shift under the pointer

Hiding a row under the user's tap caused cascading mis-taps on mobile. So checking a card must never shift the layout under the pointer.

- Under "Hide received", a checked row stays visible. It is "sticky". It stays until its package is fully received, or until the filters change. The resume reset clears sticky rows too. That is why the reset waits out `RESUME_RESET_MS`, rather than firing on every glance away.
- Sort order is snapshotted (frozen) while checking. It is recomputed only on a sort or filter change.
- Packages never auto-collapse mid-interaction.
- In the Tally view, the sticky rule is stricter. A *completed item* also stays until the filters change. Most items have a single copy. If it vanished on every tap, the mis-tap cascade would return.
- The card itself must not change height on that tap. See `card-the-action-row-is-reserved` (why the action row has a fixed height) and `card-reserved-geometry` (the measured card heights). The rows inside a package are as much under the thumb as the list is. Before that was fixed, the first check-in in a package moved every remaining row in it down by 28px.
- A remote reply never lands under a press, and never moves the row under the pointer when it lands. It waits for the press and its tail, and the page scrolls to keep the row in place. Hover and scroll are not a press (owner ruling 2026-10-20: "hover doesn't count"). The one ruled exception is the first reply after a pasted key is saved, which applies at once (`sync-revamp-cloudflare`). See `auto-apply-happens-now`.

A row moving under the thumb is the same failure this rule exists to stop.

**Pinned by:** 44.2–44.7 (the bar is out of flow, and both cards are containing blocks for it; the action row keeps its reserved height; the bar unmounts on completion), 42.1–42.2 (the save slot reserves its width and is never empty), 12.8–12.9 and 40.3 (a glance away keeps the choice and does not merge), 75.1 to 75.6 and 75.7a to 75.7c (the press guard: hover, scroll, a lost pointerup, a cancel, a key press, a cold open, a press during the photo downloads), and `npm run check:screens` (claim 2: each check-in in each card moves no other row, through completion, at 375px and 760px, in Packages and Tally; claim 16: a remote reply lands without moving the row in view or under the pointer, in Packages and Tally, with a mouse at 375px and 760px and with touch at 375px). Also not asserted: 320px, where the Tally card still moves its rows 14px on check-ins 1 and 6 (see `card-reserved-geometry`). Not asserted either: the scroll correction in WebKit.
