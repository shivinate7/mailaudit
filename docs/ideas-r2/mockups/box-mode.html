Design choices

  1. Results freeze until the next keystroke. A tick dims its row and adds a check inside the same 64-point box. The result list is taken once per keystroke, so no row moves, leaves or resizes under the pointer. Gain: no mis-taps on a fast bench pass. Loss: ticked rows stay in view on a long list. Pick: freeze (plan step 4).
  2. The live counter carries progress. A fixed three-cell counter (ticked, sellers, cost) sits above the results, with tabular figures so its width never changes. Gain: progress shows on a small phone where dimmed rows hide it. Loss: one row of height. Pick: keep.
  3. Search sits at the bottom on the phone poses and at the top on web. Gain: the main input is in thumb reach on the phone, and the web panel follows a desk reader's eye. Loss: two placements to build. Pick: split by platform. On web, Enter ticks the line when the search leaves exactly one. The keyboard is not drawn; the field rides above it.
  4. Two sellers for one name: the space is taken at the keystroke. The plan says the row "expands inline". Here the two choices show as soon as the search finds them, so the tap that confirms moves nothing. The older order is preselected (plan owner decision 2). The "Two sellers" control above shows the other option, nothing preselected. Departs from: the plan's wording "the row expands inline", not from any app behaviour.
  5. "Finish" opens the summary; "Close box" writes. One act, one name. Finish at 0 ticks discards the box and writes nothing.
  6. Surplus stays in the box summary. It never goes to Orphaned and never attaches to an order (plan owner decision 3).
  7. Slice 2 needs a ruling. The "via B-07" tag, the split-package line and Undo box need boxId and boxes[] in the saved state (invariant saved-state-shape). Needs an owner grant: yes. Slice 1 (batch check-in) writes only existing fields and needs none.
  8. Dormant entry point. "Open a box" lives in the Packages overflow menu. While a box is open on web, a "Box B-07 open" pill shows in the masthead (plan owner decision 5).
  9. The unfolded pose is its own layout. Packages on the left fills in live as ticks land on the right. It is not the folded layout made wider.
  10. Lost-mail badge drawn at 28 days. The sample needs a number; the app's own lost-mail rule applies when built.
