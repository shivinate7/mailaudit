How it builds on what exists

  • Needs mail drop: the preview email comes through the same forwarding address, by a second rule.
  • The lost-mail warning reasons from elapsed time. This adds earlier evidence: "not seen in any preview since shipped".
  • An unmatched envelope that arrives goes to Orphaned with its image. A match is only a suggestion.
  • A matched image joins that package's photos.
  • Known gap: the preview covers machine-scanned letter mail only.

Owner decisions the plan lists (its recommendation in bold)

  1. Keep images of unmatched mail? No; delete within 24 hours (the preview includes other household members).
  2. What may a match change? Only "expected today" and "sighted"; never a tick, never an orphan move.
  3. Where does text reading run? The server; images deleted once matched or after 24 hours.
  4. Does "not sighted" move the lost-mail warning? Evidence line only, until a month of data measures it.
  5. How is the preview reached? The forwarded email only, never the postal website.

Smallest first slice

  • No text reading. Show today's count and thumbnails. The owner taps one and picks the seller. The tool records the sighting and learns that return address.
