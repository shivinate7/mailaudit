Ask Box

Mockup, not built. Type a question about your orders. The box shows how it read it, answers from your own ledger with a receipt, and refuses by name when the records can't settle it.

How to use. Type into the box and press Enter, or tap an example. Try: "did the ember drake come", "did the ember drak arrive", "did the ember drake from last tuesday come", "what's late", "is a moss golem coming", "what did I spend per set". Tap a chip under "Read as" to change it to another value from the ledger. Tap an answer row to open it in Packages.
  1. Frames are illustrative: the phone is drawn at about 390 by 844, the fold as two such screens, and the web at 1180 wide. No size was read from the repo's device list.
  2. The input sits at the bottom of the phone sheet so it rides above the keyboard, in thumb reach. On the web it sits at the top of a side sheet, and / opens it.
  3. On the fold the ask box takes the right screen and Packages stays on the left, so a tapped row shows without closing the answer.
  4. "Last Tuesday" resolves to the most recent Tuesday before today (Tue 17 Nov). That is after the newest import, so it draws refusal R4. The plan's example table resolves it to Tue 10 Nov; the owner picks one.
  5. The lost-mail threshold is drawn at 21 days. The real rule was not read for this mockup.
