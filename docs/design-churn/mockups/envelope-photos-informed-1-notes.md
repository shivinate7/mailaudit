# Envelope photos: notes (informed designer 1)

Mockup: `envelope-photos-informed-1.html`. Designer kind: informed. Light theme only, because `native/Manifest/Info.plist` holds the app to Light.

**The pain.** An envelope with no readable sender sits on Orphaned until the owner works out who sent it. "I'll figure out who sent it later, I just need to remember what it looked like." The native app shows no photo and has no camera (debts `native-envelope-has-no-photos` and `native-photo-screens-unwired`). The web composer takes photos, so the photo behaviour exists already and the native port has none.

**What was read.** These records: `orphan-entry-takes-one-input`, `photo-thumbnails-and-viewer`, `label-photos-downscale-to-2000px`, `photos-stay-out-of-the-ledger`, `photos-in-git-are-permanent`, `orphaned-colours-by-meaning`, `orphaned-mail-never-decides`, `no-layout-shift-under-pointer`, `no-native-dialogs`, `ux-house-rules-govern`, `duo-card-in-hand`, `composer-fields-two-lines`, `envelope-deletion-unrepresented`, and the measurement `duo-fold-and-keyboard`. This code: `native/Manifest/OrphanedView.swift`, `native/Manifest/PhotoViews.swift`, `native/Manifest/Theme.swift`, `ManifestKit/PhotoCapture.swift`, and `PhotoStrip`, `PhotoViewer`, `EnvelopeComposer` and `EnvelopeCard` in `src/app.jsx`. The tokens are `shared/tokens.json`. The brief named the photo mockups in `docs/design/`, but none exist. The nearest is `orphaned-meta-line-wraps.html`. This mockup reuses its tokens, its frame and its Today/New marks.

**Apple pages read 2026-11-21.** Each answered 200, through the `tutorials/data` JSON. Layout (safe areas, size classes). Typography: the Dynamic Type tables at Large (Body 17, Footnote 13, Caption 1 12), at xxxLarge (Body 23) and at AX5 (Body 53, Footnote 44, Caption 1 43). Accessibility: 44x44 pt default, 28x28 pt minimum. Buttons: a 44x44 pt hit region. Sheets: Cancel on the leading edge, Done on the trailing edge. Toolbars. Tab bars: the bar floats at the bottom.

**Devices.** `layout.py devices` gives no size for any device. The folded Duo is drawn at 466 x 678 pt and the open Duo at 951 x 669 pt, with the hinge from 455.5 to 495.5 pt. Both figures come from the brief, and they match `duo-fold-and-keyboard`. The large phone has no written size in the device list or in the brief. It is drawn at 466 x 678 pt, as the same one-page compact layout. The tab bar on the open Duo is drawn 83 pt tall, a figure taken from the folded reading; the open height is unmeasured. The keys are 289 pt on the folded Duo and 264 pt on the open Duo, drawn from y 405, as `duo-fold-and-keyboard` says.

## Concepts

1. **Label strip (close to today).** Every envelope card gets a 56 pt strip under its note. The add tile comes first, then the photos. On the open Duo, each cell on the left page carries a 56 pt photo mark with a count, and the right page shows the full card with its strip. Gain: the smallest change to `OrphanedView.EnvelopeCard`, and it ports the web strip. Loss: on the folded Duo the strip sits halfway down a tall card, so a scan of the pile still means scrolling past the candidates. Pick: yes, as the safe build.
2. **Envelope faces (bold).** The pile becomes a contact sheet: two columns of envelope prints, and one column at accessibility sizes. An envelope with no photo shows a ruled card with its card names. A tap opens an envelope page with the photo leading, then Add a photo, the card and the candidates. A floating "Photograph an envelope" button sits above the tab bar. On the open Duo, the sheet is on the left page and the envelope page on the right. Gain: "which ones have pictures" is answered at a glance, and the photo is how the owner remembers the envelope. Loss: the candidates leave the folded pile, so matching takes one more tap per envelope. Owner grant: yes. Departs from: `OrphanedView.list`, which draws each full `EnvelopeCard` with its candidates in the pile. Pick: show it. It answers the brief's glance need best.

## Choices

3. **The add tile comes first in the strip, on the card and in the composer.** It is 56 pt, dashed, and carries a camera glyph. Its VoiceOver name is "Add a photo of the label", or "Take a photo of the label" in the composer. Gain: the tile never moves between shots, so a second capture hits the same spot. The order matches the web, where the button sits before `PhotoStrip` in `EnvelopeComposer`. Loss: the first tile is a control, not a photo. Pick: yes.
4. **Add a photo to a saved card (Label strip).** Gain: one tap from the pile to the camera, with no Edit first. `PhotoCapture.add` already takes a saved, live envelope. Loss: the web has no add control on a saved card. Departs from: `src/app.jsx` `EnvelopeCard`, where only `EnvelopeComposer` adds a photo. Not blocking. Pick: yes. The mockup has a control to hide it.
5. **The strip scrolls sideways and does not wrap. Its row is 56 pt tall even with no photo.** Gain: adding a photo moves nothing under the thumb (`no-layout-shift-under-pointer`). Loss: a fourth photo needs a sideways swipe. Departs from: `src/app.jsx` `PhotoStrip`, which uses `flexWrap: wrap`. The native `PhotoStrip` already scrolls sideways. Pick: yes.
6. **Camera only, through the system camera.** This keeps the web's `capture="environment"`: Retake or Use Photo, then back to the composer. Gain: one tap from the envelope in hand to the label captured. Loss: no pick from the photo library. On the open Duo the camera is drawn on the right page only, and where iOS draws it there is unmeasured. Pick: yes.
7. **Saving tile.** After Use Photo, the tile fills manila with a spinner until the blob is stored. Manila as a fill while a photo loads is ruled (`orphaned-colours-by-meaning`). Pick: yes.
8. **Photo error lines.** One manila line under the strip says "Camera access is off for MANIFEST." with Open Settings. Another says "Photo not saved: the phone is out of space." with Try again. Gain: no dead end and no silent loss. Loss: one more state to build. Departs from: `src/app.jsx` `EnvelopeComposer.addPhoto`, which swallows the error. Not blocking. Pick: yes. The mockup has a control to hide it.
9. **Viewer: zoom, pan, swipe, page dots and Done.** One tap still closes the viewer, as ruled, after the short wait for a double tap. A double tap zooms to 2.5x at the point. A pinch zooms from 1x to 4x, and a drag pans. A swipe or the dots move between photos. Done sits on the trailing edge, from the sheets page. Gain: the owner can read a postmark or a tracking number. Loss: a single tap waits for the double-tap window before it closes. Owner grant: yes. Departs from: `photo-thumbnails-and-viewer`, under which the picture is the only thing to reach. Pick: yes.
10. **Remove sits in the viewer, as a two-tap ask.** The first press reads "Remove photo". The second reads "Tap again to remove this photo", beside Cancel. Cancel takes first focus, and the ask lapses after 4 s, as Discard does. Both buttons are red on red-soft, which passes contrast on the scrim (`orphaned-colours-by-meaning`: red is a delete). Departs from: `src/app.jsx` `PhotoStrip` `onRemove`, a 22 px ✕ that removes with no ask and no undo (ux-ask-or-undo, and the 44 pt floor). Label strip removes a photo only when the viewer opens from the composer, as the web does. Envelope faces removes a photo from any envelope page, which writes the ledger at once. Owner grant: yes, for Envelope faces. Pick: yes.
11. **Line under the armed Remove: "It leaves this envelope. A copy already sent to the backup stays there."** Gain: true under `photos-in-git-are-permanent`, which has no delete path. Loss: one more line, and it may worry the owner. Pick: yes. The mockup has a control to hide it.
12. **On the open Duo, the viewer uses the left page.** The left page lists the envelope's photos large, with its cards and note. The right page holds the picture and its controls. Nothing crosses the hinge. Gain: the owner picks a photo and reads it side by side. Loss: the tab bar is covered while the viewer is open. Pick: yes. The mockup has a control to hide it.
13. **Lost photo.** Ported from the web: a manila "Lost" tile that takes no tap. Its VoiceOver name is the full sentence (`pull-warns-on-what-it-drops`). On Envelope faces, a lost first photo shows a "Photo lost" face. Pick: yes.
14. **Floating "Photograph an envelope" button (Envelope faces).** It sits above the tab bar, in the thumb zone. The precedent is the web's Find pill (`phone-nav-is-bottom-bar`). It hides while the keys are up and on the envelope page. Its text stops growing at xxxLarge, as item 104 a ruled for the post bar. The scroll area pads below it, so no print hides under it. Pick: yes. The mockup has a control to hide it.
15. **Photo first (Envelope faces).** The camera opens first. The composer then opens with the photo leading and the card field focused. "Type cards only" keeps today's composer. Pick: yes.
16. **Save with a photo and no cards (Envelope faces).** Gain: it matches "I just need to remember what it looked like". Loss: an envelope with no cards ranks no candidate. A live envelope with empty `entries` looks like a discard tombstone, which empties `entries` and `photos` and sets `deletedAt` (`envelope-deletion-unrepresented`, `saved-state-shape`). Its Discard ask would read "discard 0 cards". Owner grant: yes. Departs from: `OrphanedView.ComposerView` `ready = !d.entries.isEmpty`, and the disabled Save in `EnvelopeComposer`. Pick: show it, and let the owner rule. The mockup has a control to hide it.
17. **Photo beside the composer on the open Duo (Envelope faces).** The composer is on the left page. The new photo is on the right page, above y 405, so it stays clear of the keys. Gain: the owner reads the label while typing the cards. Pick: yes. The mockup has a control to hide it.
18. **Undo notice (Envelope faces).** After "This one", the folded envelope page closes, and the green Undo notice sits above the sheet. Departs from: `OrphanedView` `UndoNoticeView`, which sits at the envelope's old index. A notice at an index inside a grid would split a row. Pick: yes.
19. **AX5 text.** The tiles stay 56 pt, as ruled. The card's micro header breaks letter by letter at AX5. That is today's line (`orphaned-meta-line-wraps`, item 56), and these concepts do not change it. Envelope faces drops to one column. The viewer chrome uses the system font, and its text should stop growing at xxxLarge with the large-content viewer, as in item 104 a. The mockup draws the viewer chrome at the default size. Pick: yes.
20. **Keys across the hinge.** The system keyboard spans both pages on the open Duo: item 22 of `duo-card-in-hand` is provisional. The brief's "nothing may sit across the hinge" holds for app content only. Pick: unchanged.
21. **Haptics (ux-haptics-feel-substantial).** The confirmed Remove gives one haptic. The shutter's haptic is the system's own. Pick: yes.
22. **No limit on photos per envelope.** The brief says "a few", and no record sets a limit. The Worker refuses one photo over its size limit (`PhotoSync` `tooBig`). Pick: no limit.
23. **Envelope page with no cards yet (Envelope faces).** In place of the candidates it reads "No cards recorded yet. Add them with Edit, and the packages they could have come from show here." Gain: no false "no package accounts for these" line, and a way on. Pick: yes, with item 16.
24. **A print with no photo shows its note, or its card names when the note is empty.** Gain: the note ("pink bubble mailer") is the owner's own memory of how the envelope looked. Pick: yes.

## Builder needs (product code, outside the design folder)

- `PhotoCapture.add` refuses an envelope that is not saved and live. A photo taken in the composer of a new envelope needs a draft path, as the web keeps draft photo ids (`EnvelopeComposer`).
- Info.plist has no `NSCameraUsageDescription` (`native-photo-screens-unwired`).
- Removing a photo from a saved envelope needs a ledger write that drops the id and keeps the blob for the sweep.
- `check_ios_mockup.py` faults the Cochin content font, which `typography-cochin-and-mono` rules for words. It faults the existing `orphaned-meta-line-wraps.html` the same way. This may be a false alarm: the house tokens put brand fonts in content.
