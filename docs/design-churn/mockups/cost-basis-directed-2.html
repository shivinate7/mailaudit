Cost basis statement

Designer kind: directed   Direction: statement: a ledger-style statement you can export and print

What did I actually pay for what I actually got? Two concepts answer it as a statement of account. In Statement in Tally, the Tally view itself is set as the statement: one line per card name with what arrived, what is still missing and the basis, and a sum at the foot. In Statement document, Tally keeps today's rows, and a new screen shows the printed statement page by page, with totals carried forward from page to page. Both share one way out: a Share button that hands a PDF or a CSV to the iOS share sheet. Every tap on the frames works. A check-in in any frame changes every figure in every frame.

How to read the frames

  • Today: part marks a part that exists now, drawn as built. New: part marks what the concept adds. The Labels button turns them off.
  • Frames: the Duo folded (466 by 678 pt), the Duo open (951 by 669 pt, the hinge strip at 455.5 to 495.5 pt holds nothing), a large phone (375 by 678 pt, a stand-in size, see the notes), and the folded Duo at the largest accessibility text size (AX5).
  • Tap a card line to open it. Tap a box to check a copy in, or use minus and plus on a copy of two or more. Tap the Share button, then a file, then Save to Files: the CSV downloads for real, and Print opens this browser's print dialog with the full statement.
  • The period is today's Orders from range. The year chips set it to By month, January to December. Nothing new is saved.
