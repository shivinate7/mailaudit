Correcting dates and stamps

Designer kind: directed. Direction: list-first: correct from the history list, no calendar.

The brief says "history list"; in this app that list is Recent, the log of single actions. History is the list of saved versions, and this page does not touch it. Every date is picked from a list of days the ledger already knows. No calendar grid appears anywhere. Each frame works: tap a row, a day, a stamp kind, then Apply and Undo. Light theme only, because the app ships light only.

Correction slip: each Recent row opens its own correction

You find the act in Recent, where you already go to undo a mis-tap. A tap on the row (not on Undo) opens a slip for that one act. The slip lists the days that act may move to, newest first, each with what else happened that day, so you know the day by its mail. The manila slip at the foot says exactly what changes before you apply it. Apply, and the same row offers Undo.

Day book: the days are the list, and you move things between them

The Ledger tab gains a Day book: every day of the last two weeks, newest first, with what came in the mail that day and the stamps dated that day. A day with no mail still has its line, so it can take a move. Tap a package or a stamp to lift it; each day then shows a Move here target in a slot it always had. The tray at the foot says what will change. The same list, switched to Order days, picks a range by tapping its two ends.
