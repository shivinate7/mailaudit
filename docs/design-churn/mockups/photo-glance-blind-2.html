Envelope photos: whose is this?

Designer kind: blind. Designer photo-glance-blind-2, drawn 21 Nov 2026. Three concepts for one job: look at one envelope photo large enough to read, step to the next, and know whose envelope it is and which cards came in it.

Lightbox

The photo is the whole screen. Swipe or press an arrow to step. A plate under the thumb names the sender, the day and the cards, so nothing needs a tap to know whose it is.
