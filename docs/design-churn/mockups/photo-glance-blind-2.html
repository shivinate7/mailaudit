Envelope photos: whose is this?

Designer kind: blind. Designer photo-glance-blind-2, drawn 21 Nov 2026. Three concepts for one job: look at one envelope photo large enough to read, step to the next, and know whose envelope it is and which cards came in it.

Lightbox

The photo is the whole screen. Swipe or press an arrow to step. A plate under the thumb names the sender, the day and the cards, so nothing needs a tap to know whose it is.

Mail log

The first screen is a list by day. Each row shows the envelope cut down to its return address, so the sender's own writing is readable before any tap. Tap a row to open the photo; Previous and Next step from there.

Sorting dial

Bold option

A ruler of the mail sits under the thumb: one notch per photo, notches of one envelope grouped in a band, days cut by a tall line. Drag it, or tap a notch. The sender's name stands over the needle. Press and hold the photo, or turn on the loupe, to read the writing without losing your place.
