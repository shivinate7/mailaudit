Route slip

A slip under each tracked card's header: the carrier, a one-line verdict, the time of the latest step, and a track of five carrier stations plus your own. Press the slip for the full timeline: a sheet on one page, the right page when the Duo is open.

  • Gain: the verdict sits on the card you check in, so a delivered package and its check boxes are one look apart. No new page.
  • Lose: the packages that need you are spread through the list in today's sort; you scroll to find them. Each tracked card grows by one 52 pt row, so the card's reserved geometry (measurement card-reserved-geometry) must be taken again. The open Duo must spread Packages over two pages.
  • Departs from: PackagesView.PackageRow (header, then StampSection); the slip goes between them. And from DuoSpread.spreads(tab:), where .packages keeps one page.

Route board (pick)

A Tracked mail box on Packages, built like Card in Hand's Past 14 days box, opens a page of tracked packages that are not yet in hand. Groups run Delivered, not here; Returned; Stuck; then On its way. Each row draws its route on one time axis, shared by every row: order day (tick), scans (dots), the dashed wait since the last scan, today (accent line). Press a row for its timeline.

  • Gain: what needs you is at the top, counted on Packages before you open it. Each verdict word shows once, as its group's head. The shared axis makes "stuck" a shape: the longest dashed tail. The card and its reserved geometry stay as they are.
  • Lose: one press more than the slip, and the board is a second place that lists a package. A package's check-in still happens on its card, not here.
  • Departs from: HandScreen.transitBox and PastTransitPage, whose pattern it copies (a box with Open, then a page with Back); it adds a page and a DuoTab case that spreads.

Phone alert

An iOS notification when a package turns Stuck or Returned, and at 7 pm on a day the carrier said Delivered and nothing is checked in. It adds to either option above; the alert opens that package's timeline. "I want to know today" is met without opening the app.

  • Gain: the warning comes to the person; the claim window cannot close while the app sits shut.
  • Lose: it needs the checks to run with the app closed (the Worker, then a push), and a new permission ask. The 7 pm hour is a guess for the owner to set.
  • Departs from: the app sends no notification today.
