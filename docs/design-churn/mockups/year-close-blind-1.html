Year shelf

A deliberate close. Done orders stay in Orders until you put the year away. A put-away year lives on its own shelf, Years, read only, with search and export.

Receipt roll

The bold one. No lists and no tabs: each year is one printed roll. Arrived orders print onto the roll, orders still coming wait as slips under it. Closing the year tears the roll off, and it hangs above as a stub with its total. The past sits above the present.

Self-sorting

No close act at all. An order leaves the daily list the moment its last item arrives and sinks into its year. A year locks itself once it has ended and its last order is in.
