Sync line

One line under every title answers "do they agree?" in words. Tap it to reach Sync, where each merge lists what changed and keeps the older copy one tap away. Moves: a tab bar with Items, Counts and Sync. First screen: Items.

Seam

Bold: the screen is the two copies side by side, joined by a seam. Each row shows what this phone holds and what the laptop holds; the seam mark between them says whether they agree. Open the Duo and each screen holds one device's copy, with the hinge as the seam. Moves: no tab bar; you move in time, from one sync to the next, and tap a row for its older copy.
