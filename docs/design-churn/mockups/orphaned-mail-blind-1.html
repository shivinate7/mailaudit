Unknown envelope: two concepts

Designer kind: blind. The job: "I have an envelope, I do not know whose it is, and I do not want to lose it." Each concept is drawn on the folded Duo, the folded Duo at the largest text size, the open Duo, and a large phone. Every press works.

Theme

Shelf

New: whole screen

A list of kept envelopes. Open one to see what you know about it and who could have sent it, ranked by how many clues each open order shares. Wrong clues only lower a rank; nothing is struck out.

Sieve

New: whole screen

The bold one. Every open order sits on a board in a fixed place. Each clue you add turns over the orders it rules out, until one is left. Before you add a clue, it shows how many orders it would leave. Envelopes you keep sit in a strip along the top.
