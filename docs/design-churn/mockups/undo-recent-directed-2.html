Day tape

The day is a ruled tape in a tray above the bar, one tick per action. Drag it back to the first wrong tap: every row since then lights up in place, and one bar undoes them all. Each row keeps its own UNDO, one tap, as ruled.

Audit sheet

The log is a ruled sheet with a who column: a mark for the device that made each action. Switch days at the top. Press Select, tick rows (or every live row, or every row of the same order), and one bar undoes the ticked rows. On the open Duo, a tap on a row shows its full entry on the right page.

Bulk undo: arm first, or run at once

Shown on Day tape, folded. The same choice applies to Audit sheet. A single row's UNDO never arms (ruled: one tap, no arm).
