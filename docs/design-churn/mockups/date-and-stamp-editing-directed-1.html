Date and stamp editing

Designer kind: directed. Direction: calendar-first: pick on a calendar, see the effect live.

Three corrections: the day a package was marked received, the range of order dates in view, and a stamp set wrongly. Each concept below draws all three. Every tap works. Nothing is saved until Save, Move or Show, and each one leaves an Undo where you pressed. Light theme only: the app and the native client ship light (design-parchment-ledger).

Calendar sheet

Tap the value you want to correct. A sheet opens on a month with that value marked. Tap the right day: the correction writes itself under the month, the old value struck, the new one beside it, with what else it changes. On the open Duo the editor takes the right page and the left page shows the package changing as you tap.

Ledger calendar

The month is the surface. Each day carries its marks: an order placed, cards received, a stamp set. Tap a day to read it. Pick a mark up and tap the day it belongs on. The month sits in the thumb's reach, with the one action bar right above it. On the open Duo the month fills the left page and the day's ledger fills the right.
