Seller trouble

Designer kind: blind. Designer seller-trouble-blind-1. The job: "Who keeps burning me?" Two ways to answer it. Each draws the folded Duo, the folded Duo at the largest text size, the open Duo and a large phone.

Strike strip

A ranked list of sellers. Each row shows one tile per order, and the tile's shape says what went wrong. Tabs hold the sellers you stopped and the complaints you drafted.

Burn map

One field, no tabs. Each seller is a dot: how long their mail takes, against how many cards never came right. You drag your own line for "slow", and the dots past it are the ones burning you.
