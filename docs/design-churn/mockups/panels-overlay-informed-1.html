Panels over the ledger: nothing under your finger moves

Designer kind: informed. Designer panels-overlay-informed-1.

The pain: “Everything jumps when I open a menu.” Today a panel on the Ledger tab mounts in the list and pushes every row under it down. Each frame below is live: tap a row to open its panel, pick, tap outside to close. Under each frame a line reads how far any row moved on the last tap. Ruled and kept: panels overlay the list (owner, 2026-10-11), paper fill for Sorted by and History (item 63 a), one panel open at a time, a 220 ms fade with a 4 pt move.

Today: the panel pushes the list

Native LedgerTab.syncRow and history mount the panel inside the Data card, in flow. The web's Sorted by and Orders from panels do the same (panelWrap in src/app.jsx). Not an option: drawn so the jump can be felt.

Laid sheet

The panel is a sheet of paper laid on the list, hanging from the row you tapped. The rows under it stay where they were. Near the bottom it hangs upward instead, so the row you tapped stays in sight. A tap anywhere outside closes it and presses nothing. On the open Duo the Ledger takes both pages, and a sheet stays on its own page.

Facing page (bold)

On one screen the panel rises from the bottom edge, above the tab bar, where the thumb already is. Swipe it down, tap Done, or tap outside. On the open Duo there is no overlay at all: the left page holds the rows and the right page becomes the open panel, like the facing page of a ledger. Nothing on the left page is ever covered.
