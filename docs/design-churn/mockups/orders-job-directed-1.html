Orders lamp: did my new orders come in, or is it broken?

Designer kind: directed. Direction: status-light: a glanceable state and one button.

The orders status screen (unbuilt, debt orders-job-unbuilt). The phone reads GET /orders/status from the Worker; the pull itself runs in Chrome on the computer. Three concepts, each drawn on the iPhone Duo folded (466 x 678 pt), open (951 x 669 pt, fold x 455.5 to 495.5), the large phone (size not written, drawn at 466 x 678 pt), and the folded pose at AX5. Light only: the app ships one palette. Every lamp, row, button, sheet and disclosure works: press them.

Ledger row

The lamp is a row at the top of the Ledger tab's Data group, beside Sync. Its value is the lamp and one word. A press opens the panel in place, as Sync opens: one sentence, the one button, the particulars. Closest to today's Ledger tab; you see it only on the Ledger tab.

Signal screen

The Orders row pushes a screen of its own. The lamp is the hero, 120 pt, with one sentence under it. The one button is pinned above the tab bar, in the thumb's reach. Open, the left page is the signal and the right page holds the particulars.

Letterhead lamp

A small lamp sits on the masthead of every working screen, at the end of the seal's rule, so the answer shows while you check in mail. A press opens a sheet with the sentence and the one button. The most glanceable; it changes the masthead.

The one button

What the one button does, drawn on the Signal screen, folded, in each option's own state. Pull from phone asks the computer to pull, and needs a Worker and extension change. Check only reads the status again, and runs on today's Worker.
