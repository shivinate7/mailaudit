Mail day flow

Designer kind: blind. Designer mail-day-flow-blind-2. Two concepts for the whole mail day: bring the post in, sort it into piles, open each envelope, and finish with everything accounted for. Every tap works. Each concept keeps its place when you leave and come back.

Pile by pile

New: whole flow

A guided run in four steps: count in, sort by first letter into four piles, work one pile at a time, done. A strip of the four piles is always on screen, so the person sees how much is left. Set aside is a tab.

Letter board

New: whole flow

The bold one. The home screen is an alphabet. The letters that have mail today are lit, and the person sorts the post by those letters on the table. Each key shows a dot per envelope, and the dot fills when it is opened. No list, no tabs, no steps: the board is the pile, the progress and the record.
