Record an envelope with one thumb

Designer kind: directed. Direction: one-handed thumb zone: every action sits in thumb reach, header always visible.

"I keep losing the envelope I'm in." Each concept below keeps the envelope's head on screen in every state, and moves every press (match, Save, throw away, Cancel) to the bottom band above the keys. Every frame is live: type on the drawn keys, tap a match, step a count, drag the list to hide the keys, arm and confirm a throw-away, then record again to see that nothing came back.

Thumb dock

The composer becomes its own page. The envelope head is pinned at the top and never moves. The card-name field docks at the bottom like a message field, matches stack upward from it, and Save, Cancel and Throw away sit in one strip on the keys. The throw-away ask rises up from the strip, so it can never reach the head.

  • New: pinned envelope head, with the card count in it (the count shows once, here).
  • New: field and matches docked on the keys; matches overlay the list, so no line moves when they open.
  • New: after a tap, the matches hold until the next key, as Card in Hand holds them.
  • New: thumb strip (Throw away, Cancel, Save) on the keys; the ask rises from it. A second tap on Throw away keeps the draft.
  • New: "+ Record an envelope" sits at the bottom of the Orphaned list, above the tab bar.
  • Today: Cancel keeps the draft; Note, Photo of the label and Add as typed stay.

Pinned head and shelf

The composer stays in the Orphaned list, as today. When it opens, its head takes over the running head and stays pinned. Matches turn into a shelf of chips right above the keys, beside one glass bar with the count, Cancel, Throw away and Save. The throw-away ask arms inside the bar itself.

  • New: the running head names the envelope while the composer is open.
  • New: matches as a sideways shelf of chips over the keys, in the spot the thumb already types from.
  • New: one glass bar on the keys; with the keys hidden it rests above the tab bar.
  • New: Throw away arms in the bar: the pressed spot turns to Keep, the confirm takes the count's place, Save waits.
  • Today: the composer card, its field at the top, and "+ Record an envelope" at the top of the list.

Today, for reference

Today the composer sits in the list under the masthead. On focus the page scrolls to the Save row, so the head scrolls off the top; the keys cover the tab bar; and Cancel is the only way out of a new draft that is not Save.
