# Sync trust, directed designer 1: notes

Designer kind: directed. Direction: quiet confidence: a single always-visible trust mark; detail only on demand.

Mockup: `docs/design/sync-trust-directed-1.html`. It covers the native sync result, the merge notice, the kept copy, the stale masthead and Packages after a Tally check-in. Debts it answers: `native-merge-notice-unbuilt`, `native-kept-copy-has-no-route`, `native-masthead-stale-after-tabs`. Every figure on the page is a sample.

Deep links use the hash: `c` is the concept (`seal`, `countersign`, `today`), `s` is the state, and `o` is the open view (`sheet`, `list`, `history`, `keptpage`). `tab`, `fix` and `tc` drive the Tally then Packages flow. `seen=1` marks the merge as read. `h` hides options, as a comma list. `tags=0` hides the Today and New tags.

## Items

1. **A trust mark on every working screen.** One mark sits in the letterhead on Packages, Tally, Orphaned and Ledger. Gain: the question "did they agree?" has an answer on every screen, at a glance. Loss: the happy path gets a pixel it does not have today. Pick: yes. Departs from: `ruled-head-one-advisory-entrance` ("On the happy path, no sync vocabulary appears on the working screens"). The Seal adds no words, so it keeps the letter of that rule. The Countersign adds words, so it breaks it. Owner grant: yes.

2. **Concept: The Seal (pick).** The wax seal (`WaxSeal` in `native/Manifest/MastheadView.swift`) becomes the mark and a button. Whole means this device matched the server, and every tab reads the same counts. Gold ribbon tails mean a merge came in that you have not opened. A dashed, split outline in manilaInk means not backed up for a day, or the server could not be reached. A dashed outline with no split means not synced yet, or this screen could not read the latest counts. Gain: no new element, and a seal already reads as "checked and closed". Loss: a first-time user may not know the seal is a control (see item 15), and the ribbon is small on purpose. Departs from: `design-parchment-ledger` ("The seal is ornament, not a danger state"). The broken seal uses manilaInk, never red, as `SyncAdvisoryLine` does. Owner grant: yes.

3. **Concept: The Countersign.** A reserved slot of 15ch at the end of masthead row 1, mirroring the save slot on row 2 (`masthead-save-slot-reserved`, `SaveSlot`). It reads `synced 9:14 PM`, `merged 9:14 PM` in accent, `synced Nov 19` or `not synced` in manilaInk, and `not synced yet`. The words are the Sync panel's own (`SyncPanel.lastSyncText`, "Not synced yet"). Gain: the time is always in view. Loss: 10.5 pt text in a crowded row. At AX5 the masthead pushes it below the first screen (frame 4 with `c=countersign`), so it is not always visible there. It is capped at xxxLarge, as the post bar is (`duo-card-in-hand`, item 104 a). Pick: no, keep as the fallback. Owner grant: yes.

4. **No "sending" state.** A local change POSTs after `SYNC_POST_MS` (3 s, `sync-runs-itself`). The save slot already says saving, and `remote-sync-is-off-the-save-path` keeps the two apart. Gain: the mark does not flicker on every tap. Loss: for up to 3 s the mark says matched while a change has not left. Pick: no sending state.

5. **The detail.** A tap on the mark opens the detail. On one page it is a sheet with Done on the trailing edge (HIG Sheets). On the open Duo it takes the right page (item 13). Its title is "Sync", the name of the Ledger row, so one thing keeps one name. It opens with a verdict: "All is well." when nothing is not normal (house rule `ux-all-is-well`), or one sentence that names what is not normal. Then the ruled rows Last sync and Server, as the Sync panel has them (`LedgerTab.syncPanel`). A broken state gets one control, "Open Sync settings", which goes where the advisory line goes (`RootView.showSync`). Gain: one place answers the whole question. Loss: none known. Pick: yes.

6. **The merge notice lives in the detail.** "Last merge" shows `SyncClient.mergeNotice` word for word (the bundle's `mergeSummary`, `src/merge-rules.mjs`). The gate is unchanged: a merge that changed nothing has no notice. This closes `native-merge-notice-unbuilt`. Gain: the notice has a home, and the working screens stay quiet. Loss: you see the sentence only if you open the mark. Departs from: the web, which shows the merge sentence as a notice line until you press Dismiss (`src/app.jsx`, the `notice` passed to `applyBackup`). Owner grant: yes.

7. **Change list (option).** "Show what changed (n)" lists each item, with its seller and what happened. The Worker sends counts only (`stats`). So the list comes from a diff, on this device, of the ledger before and after `LedgerStore.adopt`, which holds both. That is a diff, not a merge, so `native-reply-waits-generation` ("no native code merges") still holds. Gain: it answers "what" and not just "how many". Loss: new code in `LedgerStore`. A new line from an import counts in the sentence and not in the list unless the diff covers `items`. Pick: yes.

8. **The copy that lost.** When a reply removes something, `LedgerStore.adopt` already saves a "before sync" milestone in History. New: "Your copy before this merge" in the detail opens History with that row marked. Restore arms in place, with Cancel in a slot kept free (`AskRow`, `no-native-dialogs`). Gain: the losing copy is one tap from the account of the merge. Loss: one more row in the detail. Pick: yes. Hide option: "Route to the copy before the merge".

9. **Removal line (option).** When a merge undid one of your check-ins, a manila line shows on the working screens: "The other device un-checked 1 card — tap to see". It goes once the detail is opened. Gain: the one merge that undoes your own work does not wait to be found. Loss: it speaks unasked, against the direction, and it is a second kind of line beside the advisory (`ruled-head-one-advisory-entrance`). Pick: yes, for removals only. Owner grant: yes.

10. **Other device's last sync (option).** A row in the detail: "Other device last synced Nov 20, 9:10 PM". Gain: it answers "did my phone and my laptop agree" in so many words. Without it, the detail can only vouch for this device. Loss: the Worker must keep the last POST time for each device and send it. That is a Worker change and a new stored field, and it needs a device id. Pick: yes, if the Worker change is granted. Owner grant: yes.

11. **Kept copy route (option).** The Packages notice line ("1 entry could not be read; the original is kept on this device.", `LedgerNotice.damagedLine`) becomes a button with "Open". The detail gets a Kept copy section too. Both open a Kept copy page: when it was kept, how many entries could not be read, its size, and "Share the kept copy", which hands the file to the share sheet as Backup does (`LedgerStore.backup`). The file name sits in a closed disclosure (`ux-machine-text-hidden`). The app does not restore from the kept copy, and the page says so. This closes `native-kept-copy-has-no-route`. Gain: the copy the notice names can be reached. Loss: the page cannot show what the unreadable entry was. Pick: yes.

12. **Packages after a Tally check-in.** Today, Packages keeps old rows after a Tally check-in, while the letterhead counts the card (debt `native-masthead-stale-after-tabs`, `PackagesModel`). New: Packages reads the ledger again when its tab shows, and after every write that moves a check-in. If that read fails, the seal goes unpressed and the detail says "Packages shows old counts." with Read again. Gain: the cause is fixed, and the mark never vouches for a screen with old numbers. Loss: one read per tab change. Pick: yes. This is a builder fix, so the mockup shows it and does not ask.

13. **Open Duo: list left, detail right.** The list stays on the left page and goes on onto the right page. The detail replaces the right page when you tap the mark, so the account sits beside the screen it vouches for. Nothing crosses the fold. Gain: the open pose uses its width (HIG Layout). Loss: Packages now spreads. Departs from: `duo-card-in-hand` ("Packages does not spread on the open Duo", default, not owner) and `PackagesTab` in `native/Manifest/HandScreen.swift`, which draws one page across the fold. Not blocking. Hide option "Detail on the right page" to see the sheet instead. Owner grant: yes.

14. **The stale case on native.** "Not backed up since Nov 19 — tap to fix" is the web's case 4 (`SYNC_STALE_MS`, 24 h). Native lacks it today: the comment on `SyncPanel.row` names the missing stale clock. Gain: parity with the web, and the broken seal has a matching line. Loss: none. Pick: yes.

15. **A second way in.** The Ledger tab's open Sync panel gets a row, "Merges and kept copies", that opens the same detail. Gain: a person who never taps the seal still finds the account. Loss: one more row in the panel. Pick: yes.

16. **Accessibility.** The seal draws at 36 pt inside a 44 pt hit region (HIG Accessibility, 44x44 pt). Its VoiceOver label says the state in words, so the ribbon's gold never carries meaning alone. It keeps its size at accessibility sizes and offers the large content viewer. At AX5 the verdict stacks the seal above its words (`ViewThatFits`). With reduced motion the ribbon is drawn with no hang (`motion-reduced-leaves-it-drawn`). Gain: the mark works with VoiceOver, at every text size and with reduced motion. Loss: the seal does not grow with the text, so at AX5 it is small beside 53 pt words. Pick: yes.

17. **Sizes and theme.** The Duo poses use the brief's sizes: 466 x 678 pt folded, 951 x 669 pt open, with the fold at 455.5 to 495.5 pt. The folded tab bar starts at y 595 pt (`duo-fold-and-keyboard`). The status bar is drawn at the 20 pt UI-test floor, which is unmeasured on the Duo. The large phone has no written size (`layout.py devices` answers unknown). It is drawn at 402 x 874 pt with a 54 pt top inset, as stand-ins, unmeasured. The native app is held to light (`design-parchment-ledger`), so every frame draws the light theme only. Gain: no size is made up for the Duo. Loss: the phone frame may be wrong by a few points, and no dark frame exists to judge. Pick: draw the phone at the stand-in until its size is written in `layout.json`.

18. **Focus ids for the waiting page.** In the folded frame: `mark` (the seal or the countersign, and today's plain seal), `advisory-line` (the removal line or the stale line), `other-sync` (the other device's row) and `detail` (the sheet). The other frames add `-open`, `-phone` or `-ax5`. On the open Duo, `detail-open` is the right page. Gain: each look-based option has a target. Loss: none. Pick: yes.

## Check

`check_ios_mockup.py` reports one fault, `system-font`: a font declaration starts with Cochin. The repo's content font is Cochin by ruling (`typography-cochin-and-mono`, `Font.cochin`), and the app chrome on this page (nav bars, tab bars) starts with `-apple-system`. The existing `duo-hand-held-box.html` gets the same fault. This may be a false alarm for an app whose content font is not the system font. The page does not route around it.
