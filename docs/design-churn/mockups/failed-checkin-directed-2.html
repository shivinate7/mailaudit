The check-in ribbon

Designer kind: directed. Direction: one ribbon: a single persistent strip with a count and retry all.

Every check-in that does not save joins one red strip. It holds the count until each one saves. A later good tap, a tab change or the stamped toggle never clears it. Two homes for the strip are drawn: the Foot ribbon above the tab bar, and the Head ribbon in the running-head slot. iPhone Duo folded (466 x 678 pt) first, then open (951 x 669 pt, fold at x 455.5 to 495.5), then a large phone (402 x 874 pt, a size no record gives). Light theme only: the native app is held to light.
