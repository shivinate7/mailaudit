Undo shelf

A shelf sits on every tab, just above the tab bar, and holds your last taps. Each has its own Undo. It never opens, never scrolls, and never moves. The working list ends above it, so nothing slides under it. Only the Undo words act. The rest of the shelf takes a tap and does nothing, so a tap that misses Undo lands on nothing and the tab bar below it is out of reach.

Recent tab

Recent becomes a fifth tab, so the list is one tap away from any tab. It is a page, not a panel: it covers nothing and pushes nothing. Each row has one Undo cell. A tap on the rest of a row shows its package, where you tick the card you meant. On the open Duo the package sits on the right page while the list stays on the left.
