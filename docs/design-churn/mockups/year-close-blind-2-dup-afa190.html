Putting a finished year away

Designer kind: blind. Designer: year-close-blind-2. Job: "Put the done stuff away, but never lose it." Each frame below is clickable. Links under each option open one state; the hash holds it (for example #c=close&s=sheet&t=dark).

Year close

Two tabs, Orders and Records. Received orders stay in Orders until the year ends; then one press puts the year's received orders into its record. Records keeps every year, its total, and every order, read only.

Strata (the bold one)

No tabs and no archive screen. One column runs through time: what is waiting sits at the bottom, by the thumb, and each year is a band above it, as tall as what it cost. A received order settles into its year's band the moment it is in, so clutter never builds. Year end only seals the band, so it can never change.
