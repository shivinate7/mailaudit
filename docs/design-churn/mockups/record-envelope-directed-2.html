Record an envelope by tapping, not typing

Designer kind: directed (record-envelope-directed-2). Direction: keyboardless: tap-to-pick from the order's own cards, typing only as a fallback.

The keys stay down until you ask for them. Every card you could be holding is already in the ledger, so you tap it. The envelope's head is pinned at the top: no key, no Save bar and no Discard ask can reach it. Everything is live: tap cards, open Type, arm Discard, switch tabs.

Packing slip

Pick the first card from an A to Z list with a letter rail at the thumb. Then Its orders opens the order slips that hold it, and you tap the rest of the envelope off those slips. The slips hold their order while you tap; a slip that explains less goes soft in place.

Card index

One flat index of every card still owed, as tiles. A to Z with a letter rail, or oldest order first. No list ever narrows or reorders while you tap. The matched lines sit behind one line under the tray: Could have come from.
