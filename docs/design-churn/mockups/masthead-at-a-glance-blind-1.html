Count strip

Two counts in one strip, then only what is wrong, then the sync line. A tab bar reaches the check-off list, the late packages and the sync record. The open Duo drops the tab bar and shows all three at once.

Plain sentence

The top of the screen reads as two or three short sentences. Each bold phrase is the way in: press it and the list below shows those packages. No tab bar. The open Duo puts the sentences on the left page and the list on the right.

Fill line

The bold one. Every package is a segment of one line, oldest first, as wide as its card count. Filled is in, hollow is out, hatched may be lost. The line is also the only way to move: drag along it and the list follows; scroll the list and a frame on the line follows. On the open Duo the line stands upright beside the hinge.
