Carrier line

Keeps today's four tabs. Each tracked package card gains one carrier line under its date line. A new Tracking cell in the ruled head names only what needs a look, and a tap shows just those cards. The carrier line opens the detail: a sheet on one screen, the right page on the open Duo.

Doorstep

The bold one. A fifth tab, Tracking, sorted by what needs you. On top sit the slips: packages the carrier says it left. Each asks one question, "It's here" or "Not here". "It's here" opens the package's cards to check in. "Not here" offers the two stamps a claim starts with. Below: stuck, returned, can't check, no number, and the ones on their way.
