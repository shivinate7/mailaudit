Undo recent ticks

Designer kind: blind. Designer: undo-recent-blind-2. Three concepts for one job: see the last several ticks and undo any of them with one tap, minutes later, without hitting the wrong control.

Shelf

The last three ticks sit on a fixed shelf above the tab bar, on the screen where the person ticks. No new screen. See all opens the full list.

Recent tab

A screen of its own, one tab away. Every tick of the day, newest first. Undo sits in its own column at the thumb edge. On the open Duo the right page shows the order, so the person can tick the right card.

Replay

Bold concept

Each tick replays the list as it looked at the moment of the tap, with a ring where the finger landed. A mis-tap is almost always the row next door, so the fix is right there: undo, or move the tick to the row you meant. Step back through taps with Earlier and Later.
