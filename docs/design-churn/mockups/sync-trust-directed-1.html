Sync trust: one mark, detail on demand

Designer kind: directed · Direction: quiet confidence: a single always-visible trust mark; detail only on demand · sync-trust-directed-1

The job: "Did my phone and my laptop actually agree?" Every working screen carries one mark in the letterhead. It never speaks while all is well, it changes shape when something is not normal, and a tap opens the whole account: when this device last matched the sync server, what the last merge brought in, what it took away, and where the losing copy is kept. Two concepts carry the mark. Each frame below is live: tap the mark, the tabs, the rows in the detail, and the check boxes on Tally.

Pick: The Seal. The letterhead already has a wax seal, and a seal is what people read as "this has been checked and closed". Making it the mark adds no new element to the working screens, and its broken shape for "not synced" reads without words. The countersign is the fallback if the owner wants the time always in view.
