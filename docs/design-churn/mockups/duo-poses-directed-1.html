Desk mode: the Duo in its stacked poses

Designer kind: directed. Direction: desk mode. The laptop pose is the primary pose. Keys and list below, answer above.

Stacked pages are the open inner screen turned a quarter: 669 by 951 pt, top page y 0 to 455.5, fold 455.5 to 495.5, bottom page 495.5 to 951. No record measures this window; the size is the brief's open size turned. Laptop is the hinge half open (DuoHinge.halfOpen), tall is flat (.flat); both report DuoSpread.stacked. The keys' height in this pose is unmeasured: they are drawn with the side-by-side frame's 263 pt plus its 44 pt drawn strip, so they start at y 644 (dashed); the dotted line at 667 is the top with no word suggestions. Light theme only: the app ships light. The laptop, tall and folded Typewriter frames are live: tap them or type on your own keyboard. Shift and 123 are drawn, not live; the other frames are references. Ruled: answer page on top, system tab bar at the bottom of the screen (item 28 a), the tab bar stays under the keys while they are up.

Pick: Typewriter. In laptop pose the top page stands up at arm's length and wobbles under a finger, and the bottom page lies under the hand. Typewriter puts every press but Post on the flat page and makes the standing page a large, quiet answer. Desk is the safer step: it keeps today's press places and only stacks the pages.

Typewriter

The standing page is the answer, read at arm's length; the flat page is the typewriter. The field sits on the fold's lower edge with New envelope and Close beside it. Each match row carries its own check box, so a card checks in where it was named, and the answer above ticks the same line. On Packages the field-shaped opener says what Card in Hand does, and an order opens its lines on the flat page. The one press left on the standing page is Post, at its lower edge, nearest the hand.

Typewriter on the folded screen (option). Card in Hand opens on the one folded page too, with the answer above the field and the keys.

Desk

Packages spreads over both stacked pages: orders below, the picked order or the day above, as a list under its detail. The field below is the Packages search and filters the list. Card in Hand opens from its own control beside the search and stacks today's two pages: the answer page above with its tappable lines and Post bar, the left page below with the keys. The check-in presses stay where they are today, on the lines of the answer page. Like Typewriter, it moves Post to the answer page's foot, the head's figures up and Close into the field row, and it adds a search.
