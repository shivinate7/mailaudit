Checklist by week

New: whole first run

One checklist, grouped by the week you ordered, because mail comes in roughly the order you bought it. The three words are taught on your own first package, and you tick your first card inside the lesson. A second tab, Imports, keeps every file you brought in and what it added, so you can see nothing was lost.

Envelope in hand

New: whole first run

Bold structure: no list and no tab bar on the first screen. The app starts from the thing you hold. One box at the thumb asks for the name on the envelope; the match opens like the envelope does, and the cards inside are the checklist. The three words are taught by opening a drawn envelope from your own file. Everything still coming sits one tap away, never in the way.
