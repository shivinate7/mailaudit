Envelope progress

Designer kind: blind. Each concept answers one job: hold an envelope from one sender, read the cards off one by one, and always know how many are found, how many remain, and whether the envelope is complete or short. Every control inside a device works. The frames of one concept share one state.

Ledger list

A checklist of the names on the envelope. A bar with one tick per card and one number sit above the list, so "how many left" is never a count. A name field sits under the thumb. A row that is done sinks to Found on the next press.

Slot tray

The envelope drawn as a tray with one card-shaped slot for each card it should hold. A found card fills its slot in place, and nothing moves. Done is a tray with no empty slot. There is no list to scroll: you type the name on the card and the answer card says which slot it fills.
