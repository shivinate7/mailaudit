Large text check-in

Designer kind: blind. Designer: large-text-blind-1.

Three concepts. Each keeps three things in view at every text size: how many cards arrived out of how many were ordered, how much money is still owed, and the main button. Tap rows and buttons in any frame: every frame of a concept shares one state.

Theme
Text size, for every frame but the AX5 one
State
Dashed labels

Thumb dock

One list, grouped by seller. The arrived count, the money still owed and the main button share one band at the bottom, in reach of the thumb. Cancel and Undo sit in the title bar, where iOS puts Cancel. Open, the sellers sit on the first screen and their cards on the second.

Fact tabs

The two facts are the tabs. Tap the money to see the cards still coming; tap the count to see the cards that arrived. The facts are the way around, so they are always on screen. The button bar sits at the bottom; at accessibility sizes Cancel moves to the title bar.

One card at a time

No list on the first screen: the facts fill it, with one button to start. Then the app asks about one card at a time, in big type, with the answers under the thumb. The list is one tap away when folded, and always open on the second screen when open.
