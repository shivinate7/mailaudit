Panels overlay the list

Designer kind: directed. Direction: popover: anchored to its button, never moves the list.

"Everything jumps when I open a menu." Each option row on the Ledger tab opens a paper popover that hangs from that row. Nothing under it moves. One gesture closes it: a pick, a tap outside, a tap on the row again, or Escape. Two concepts, each in its own state, beside today's build for reference. Tap the rows in any frame. The line under each frame measures how far the other rows moved.

How both concepts behave

  • Never moves the list. The popover is out of flow. It overlays the rows below or above its row. The rows keep their place: the readout under each frame compares every row's top with the closed state.
  • Anchored. It hangs from its row, and a notch points at the row's value. It never covers its own row (HIG Popovers). It opens below when it fits above the tab bar, else above, else on the larger side with its own scroll. The page never scrolls to make room, because a scroll is a jump.
  • One gesture closes it. A pick (single choice), a tap outside, a tap on its row, or Escape. A tap outside is used up by the close, so it never also presses Showing, Backup or Reset under it. A tap on another option row closes this popover and opens that one in the same tap (HIG Popovers).
  • One at a time. Sorted by and Orders from close each other, and History and Sync close each other, as today (history-sync-close-each-other). Now every pair does, since only one popover can stand.
  • Paper. The fill is paper (head-panels-carry-paper-fill, item 63 a), with a 1 pt line edge, 8 pt corners and a shadow, so its square-edge question goes away.
  • Arrival. 220 ms, opacity and 4 pt (motion-disclosure-panels-arrive). Reduced motion: it is simply there.
