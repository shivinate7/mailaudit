Orders: a run log

Designer kind: directed. Direction: run-log, a readable history of every run. The screen answers "Did my new orders come in, or is it broken?" by keeping every run of the orders extension as a line in a book: when it ran, who started it, what it read, what it added, and what failed and why. Silence is written down too.

Two concepts. Day book: runs as dated entries in plain sentences, a pull button over them, a run's page beside them on the open Duo. Register: runs as ruled account lines with weekly totals, under a strip of days that shows the rhythm at a glance. Press anything: Pull now, Stop, Cancel ask, an entry, a day, Back.
