Checkpoints

One history of every import and every safety copy, newest first. A file is checked in a sheet before anything changes, with the stamp and dates set there. Each import in the history shows its check-in count before and after, so the proof sits where the fear is.

Tracing paper

The first screen is the ledger itself. A file is laid over it like tracing paper: new orders are inked on the sheet, your check-ins sit on top of it, and the sheet lifts to peek under. Safety copies are a stack of earlier sheets under the ledger; restoring lays one over in the same way, and shows what it would take back. Open, the ledger and the sheet line up row for row across the hinge.
