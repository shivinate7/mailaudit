Quiet alerts

Job: speak up only when it matters, never nag. The person picks which kinds they hear about (mail likely lost, a sync that did not agree, an order overdue) and when. Three concepts, each with its own structure, movement and first screen. Every part drawn is new: as a blind designer I have not seen what exists today.

Quiet line (the bold one)

One surface, no tabs. This week's alerts sit in three bands: right away, at the round-up, and below a heavy line, kept quiet. The settings are the bands: tap a kind to move it, or tap the bell on a quiet row you would regret missing. Open, the hinge is the line.

Sentences

Two tabs. Alerts is a plain list by day; each alert opens to the one sentence that caused it. Rules is that set of sentences, with the words you can change tinted. Open, the right page shows what the rules would have told you this week.

Morning letter

The tool speaks once a day, in a short letter. Page back through past days. Only a kind you allow may break the letter and reach you right away. Open, the letter sits on the left and what goes into it on the right, so each change shows at once.
