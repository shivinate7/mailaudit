Panels as sheets

Designer kind: directed   Direction: sheet: bottom sheet with a grab handle and snap points

Every option panel rises from the bottom edge as a sheet on solid paper. The list under the finger never moves. Drag the grab handle between its snap points, tap the handle to step through them, swipe it down to close. Two options follow, each drawn on the folded Duo, the open Duo, a large phone, and the folded Duo at the largest text size.

Ledger sheet

The Ledger tab keeps its rows. A row that opens something now raises its own sheet, sized to what it holds, instead of opening in the card and pushing the rows below.

Docked sheet

The view options leave the Ledger tab and rest as a docked sheet above the tab bar on Packages and Tally. Pull it to half and the list re-sorts behind it while you watch. Swipe it down and it rests again.
