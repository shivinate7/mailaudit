Recording an envelope without losing which one you are in

Orphaned's envelope composer: type card names, choose the matching lines, then save or throw the draft away. Two options, each live in four frames that share one draft: iPhone Duo folded, the Duo open, a large phone, and the folded Duo at AX5. Tap the drawn keys or type on your keyboard. Drag the list to hide the keys. Light theme only (design-parchment-ledger). Debts: composer-keys-cover-tab-bar, native-discard-panel-covers-head, discard-keeps-draft.

Envelope sheet (the bold one)

New: the composer opens as a sheetNew: the sheet's toolbar is the headNew: the card field sits on the keys, matches rise from itNew: open Duo, envelope left, matches right

Recording becomes its own place. The sheet's toolbar says which envelope this is, with Cancel leading and Save trailing, as iOS sheets do. The envelope's cards fill the middle like a slip. The card field sits directly on the keys, under the thumb, and the matches rise out of it, best match nearest the field. Discard draft sits at the end of the slip, far from the head, and arms in place. On the open Duo the envelope takes the left page and the matches the right.

What works on this page

  • + Record an envelope, Edit, the card field, the note field, every match row, "Add … as typed", the steppers, Cancel, Save envelope, Discard draft and its ask, the envelope's Discard and its ask, the tab bar.
  • The drawn keys type, and so does your own keyboard (into the option you touched last). Return adds the typed name. A drag on the list, or a wheel turn, hides the keys in both options; Today has no way to hide them.
  • After a tap on a match, the list holds still until the next key, with the tapped row bold; tap it again to add one more.
  • An ask lapses after 4 s, as built. An ask opened from a link (#s=armed, #s=card-armed) holds until you press, so the state can be judged.
  • Under each frame, the page reads its own drawing: where the head, the asks and the keys sit, and whether anything covers the head.
