Mail day workbench

Designer kind: directed. Direction: workbench: everything visible at once, no steps. Designer: mail-day-flow-directed-2.

The whole mail day on one surface. The pile rail at the top always says how much is left. The card field, the envelope on the bench, and the trays for what is done, set aside or not normal all show at the same time. Nothing opens a step. Leave the app and come back: the bench is as you left it. Every control on the drawn screens works.

The bench

One envelope sits on the bench. Around it, at all times: the pile rail, the card field, and three trays (Done today, Set aside, Not normal). On the open Duo the left page is your hands (field, held cards, trays of what you finished), the right page is the envelope and what needs a second look.

The wall

Every envelope you are still owed is a tile on one wall, oldest order first, and no tile ever moves. Typing a card lights the tiles that hold it and dims the rest. Tap a lit tile to put it on the bench. Finished tiles turn green in place, so the wall itself shows how far the day has gone.

How the day's post is counted

Both concepts need to know how many envelopes came today, so the rail can say how many are left. Two ways to enter it. Each has its own hide button.
