Date and stamp editing: correct the day, see it first, undo it

Designer kind: informed · date-and-stamp-editing-informed-1

The pain, in the owner's words: "I marked it on the wrong day." Today the app records when a card was checked (receivedAt) but shows it nowhere and lets no one move it; a stamp's date is the day it was set, and a new kind re-dates it to today; the range picker takes months, or two native date fields. Two concepts fix it. Each is a working prototype: tap through it in any frame. Light theme only: the app and the native client are held to light.

Correction slip: fix it in the card

The quiet concept. The RECEIVED stamp gains its day and becomes a control, as the stamp band already is. Each editor gets a week of day cells and a slip that states the change before Save. After Save, a receipt line in the card and a Recent row each offer one-tap Undo. The range panel's two native date fields become a two-tap calendar that previews its count before it applies.

Daybook: every date is an entry you can pick up

The bold concept. One screen holds every date a package has, one ruled row per day, from its order to today. Tap a stamp to pick it up, tap a day to put it down. The old place stays as a struck ghost, so nothing moves. The slip lists every change waiting; Post writes them all, and one Undo takes them all back. The same rows pick a range: tap the first day, tap the last.
