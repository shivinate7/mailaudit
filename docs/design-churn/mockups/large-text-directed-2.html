Foot band

The core sits in one band, docked on the tab bar, where the thumb is. The letterhead and the list scroll above it, and the list ends clear of the band. The band tries four shapes in order and takes the first that fits: one row, a pair, ledger lines, then a stack. A figure may break only after its slash.

Core page

Open, the left page becomes the core: the letterhead, both figures at full size and the button, and none of it scrolls. The names move to the right page and open in place. Folded and on the phone, the same core is a head band that pins under the letterhead as it scrolls away, where the running head pins today. Its height never changes, so pinning moves nothing.
