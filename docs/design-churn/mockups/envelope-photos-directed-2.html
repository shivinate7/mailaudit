Contact sheet

Every unmatched envelope is one square on a sheet, newest first. A photo is the face; an envelope with no photo shows its note on a dashed index card, so the gaps stand out. Filter to the ones with or without photos, sort by date or by colour, and make the squares larger. Tap one to see its photos, cards, note and the orders it could have come from.

Stacks

The same envelopes pinned up as a wall you sort by eye. When two envelopes look like the same sender, select them and stack them; a stack fans its photos and takes a name. A stack is only for your eye: each envelope still matches its own order. On the open Duo, Compare puts two envelopes side by side, one on each page.
