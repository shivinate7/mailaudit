Mail day as one conveyor

Designer kind: directed. Direction: conveyor, a single linear path, one step at a time.

The whole mail day runs as one path: bring the post in, sort it into small piles, open each envelope, and finish when the pile is empty. Each concept below is live. Press anything inside a device. Every device shows the same moment, so a press in one moves all of them. Pause leaves the path, and the place is kept, even across a page reload.

Stations

The envelope is the unit. Four stations stay on screen as a rail: Bring In, Sort, Open, Done. The one press that moves you on always sits in the same place at the thumb. Open holds one envelope at a time; Next Envelope ends it and counts the pile down.

Card belt

The card is the unit. You count while you sort: one tap per envelope as it lands on a pile. Then each card in your hand passes the field once, and the belt above it keeps what went by. Envelope Empty cuts the belt and counts the pile down.
