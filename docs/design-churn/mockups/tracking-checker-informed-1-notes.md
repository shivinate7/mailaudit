# Tracking checker: notes for tracking-checker-informed-1

Designer kind: informed. Mockup: `docs/design/tracking-checker-informed-1.html`. Debt: `tracking-checker-unbuilt`.

**The pain.** The owner has tracking on some orders, but the app shows only "● tracked". To see where a package is, he leaves the app. A package the carrier calls delivered, but that never came, goes unseen until the claim window is nearly shut. In his words: "If it says delivered and it isn't here, I want to know today." The lost-mail flags skip tracked packages by ruling (`duo-card-in-hand`, Tracked late packages, 15:34 CDT, option a; `lostMail` in `src/view-rules.mjs`). So today nothing warns about a tracked package at all.

**Deep links.** `#c=line&s=<state>&t=light` and `#c=door&s=<state>&t=light`. Line states: `loaded`, `today`, `needs`, `sheet`, `nonumber`, `checking`, `failed`, `first`, `allwell`, `empty`. Door states: the same set without `today` and `needs`, plus `nothere`, `stamped`, `here` and `notify`. Focus ids for the waiting page: Carrier line `line-carrier`, Doorstep `door-slips`.

**Drawn.** For each concept: the Duo folded (466 x 678 pt, from the brief), the Duo open (951 x 669 pt, hinge 455.5 to 495.5, from the brief), a large phone, and the folded pose at AX5. Light theme only: the app and the native client are held to light (`design-parchment-ledger`).

## Items

1. **Concept: Carrier line.** Keeps the four tabs. Each tracked card gets one carrier line under its date line: a dot, the verdict, the carrier and the age. A tap on the line opens the detail: a sheet on one screen, the right page on the open Duo. A new Tracking cell in the ruled head names only what needs a look ("4 need a look"). A tap on the cell shows only those cards, with Show all to go back. Gain: no new place to learn; the warning sits on the card he already works from. Loss: the warning is spread across a long list; on mail day the delivered packages are not grouped. Pick: second.
2. **Concept: Doorstep (the bold one).** A fifth tab, Tracking, sorted by what needs him. On top are the slips: one for each package the carrier says it left that is not checked in. Each slip asks one question, with two big buttons: "It's here" and "Not here". It's here opens the package's cards to tick, with Mark all received. Not here offers the two stamps a claim starts with. Below the slips: Stuck, Returned, Can't check, No number yet, and a closed On its way list. On the open Duo the board is on the left page; the right page is the door, the cards the selected package should hold. Gain: answers his sentence directly, the same day, in one tap. Loss: a fifth tab, and a second way to check cards in. Pick: first. Owner grant: yes (the tab bar is ruled at four tabs, `phone-nav-is-bottom-bar`).
3. **The verdicts.** On its way, Delivered, Stuck, Returned: the brief's four words, one each, one colour each. Delivered splits on the ledger: checked in (green) or not checked in (ink for the first 24 h, then red). Three more states keep unknown apart from empty (`ux-unknown-is-not-empty`): Can't check (the carrier did not answer; the last word shows with its age), No number yet, and Not checked yet. Gain: no unknown reads as fine. Loss: seven states to learn. Pick: all seven.
4. **Stuck means no new scan for 5 days.** No ruling or record gives a figure. 5 days is my pick. 3 days would warn sooner but cry wolf on slow ground mail. Pick: 5 days, owner to tune.
5. **Delivered and not checked in turns red after 24 h.** Before that it shows in ink: the mail may simply not be opened yet. Gain: the warning is early but not false on the day itself. Loss: a day's wait for the red. Pick: 24 h.
6. **Lost-mail flags stay as ruled.** `lostMail` still skips tracked packages and still flags untracked ones at 14 and 30 days from the order date (`lost-mail-flags-use-order-date`). The tracking verdict is a separate signal, and it never feeds `lostMail`. The untracked card is drawn as today. Pick: keep.
7. **Claim by, for a tracked package, is the order date + 30 days.** This is the same conservative proxy the lost-mail flags use. TCGplayer counts 30 days from its delivery estimate, so its window can close later (the past-transit page says the same). Shown on the detail and on a stamped slip. Pick: keep the proxy. Owner grant: yes (a new rule for tracked packages; today no rule covers them).
8. **A stamp hides the tracking warning.** This extends `stamped-package-hides-lost-flag`: the order is already being handled by hand. A stamped slip keeps its slot until the next load, as the past-transit page keeps a stamped row (`PastTransitModel`, sticky). Pick: keep.
9. **"Not here" writes an existing stamp.** Seller contacted or Claim filed, with More for the other four, from `STAMP_KINDS`. No new kind and no new saved field. The Lost stamp stays deferred (`duo-card-in-hand`, item 25). The Not here row is one row of 48 pt, the height of the two big buttons, so nothing moves under the finger (`no-layout-shift-under-pointer`). Undo writes the tombstone (`stamp-removal-writes-a-tombstone`). Pick: keep.
10. **Where the tracking data lives.** The carrier, number, status and time are new data. A new ledger key touches the five sites of `saved-state-shape` and must be optional and defaulted. The other way keeps it off the ledger, in a store of its own on the device or the Worker, keyed by `gkOf`. Gain of the second: the frozen shape stays frozen, and a wrong scan can never reach the check-ins. Loss: one more store to sync. Pick: off the ledger. Owner grant: yes.
11. **Where the numbers come from.** The OrderWand CSV holds no numbers or carrier data (`orderwand-csv`). Today the drawn path is a paste: a sheet with one 16 pt field (`token-field-is-sixteen-pixels`), a Paste button, and a carrier guess from the number's shape. Two other paths need a ruling: the orders extension reads the number from the order page (unmeasured whether the page shows it), or a carrier service is queried by the Worker. That needs a carrier account and its key, which is a credential (`the-token-is-a-credential`), and may cost money. Pick: paste now, the extension next. Owner grant: yes.
12. **How the check runs.** "Passively" (the owner's word) means a check on app open and in the background when iOS allows it. There is no timer of the app's own. Check now starts a run at once; a run that takes more than a few seconds shows "Checking 3 of 8" and Stop (`ux-long-run-stops`). A failed run keeps the last word and says when the last good check was. Cadence and run time are unmeasured. Pick: keep.
13. **One notification for each delivery** (Doorstep only; it can be hidden). At the first check after the carrier says delivered: "Delivered, not checked in. Sample Seller One: USPS left it at 2:14 PM. Is it here?" A tap opens the slips. Gain: he knows the same day without opening the app. Loss: a permission prompt, and background refresh. Pick: yes, one per delivery, none for any other verdict. Owner grant: yes (new permission).
14. **The carrier line replaces "● tracked" on tracked cards** (Carrier line). The untracked card keeps "○ untracked". Departs from: `src/app.jsx` `PackageCard`, the tracked glyph after the date. Not blocking.
15. **The Tracking cell in the ruled head** (Carrier line). It is a ruled cell, not a second advisory line, so `ruled-head-one-advisory-entrance` (one manila line, for Sync only) still holds. Its value is underlined when a tap does something (`disclosure-cells-underline-value`), and plain when it says "All is well." (`ux-all-is-well`). Owner grant: yes (it adds a cell to the ruled head).
16. **Packages spreads on the open Duo** (Carrier line). The list is on the left page, the carrier detail on the right. Departs from: the default "Packages does not spread on the open Duo" (`duo-card-in-hand`, Defaults; default, not owner). Not blocking.
17. **Doorstep's check-in reuses the card lines.** The ticks are the same check-ins Packages writes, through the same writer, so the two views never disagree (`three-views-share-one-switch`). The ticks on the right page are drawn as Packages draws them. Pick: keep.
18. **Copy is plain.** The verdict sentence says what the carrier said and when. The raw carrier text shows as "Latest", in the person's words; no code or id shows. "Open at USPS" hands off to Safari. Pick: keep.
19. **AX5.** Drawn at the HIG AX5 sizes, each app size scaled by its text style: Title 2 56, Headline 53, Body 53, Footnote 44, Caption 1 43. The slip's two buttons stack full width; every row wraps; the tab labels stop at the default size, and the large content viewer shows them (ruled in `duo-card-in-hand`, Tab bar text). Pick: keep.

## Checks run

- `check_ios_mockup.py`: one fault, `system-font`, because content text is set in Cochin. This is kept: `typography-cochin-and-mono` rules Cochin for words, the native app uses it (`.cochin(...)` in `HandScreen.swift`), and house tokens allow a brand font in content. App chrome (status bar, tab bar, sheet toolbar, back buttons) uses the system font. This may be a false alarm in the check.
- `label_once.py` on the rendered frames: the repeats left are data (a seller name on the card and in its detail), the tab label that is also the page title (`ux-one-plain-name` asks for that), and today's "○ untracked" on each untracked card.
- impeccable critique (native: design review only). Fixed: a trailing "·" on the Not checked yet line; "not checked in" said twice in the detail; the open Duo's right page claimed "It's here" before any press; a bare "6 d" that could read as the order's age (now "no scan for 6 d").
