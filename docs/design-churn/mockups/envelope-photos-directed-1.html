Shutter first

One press on the shutter bar opens the camera full screen. Shoot as many as you need; each shot drops into the tray. Done takes you to the cards and the note, with the shot on top. In the pile, each envelope leads with its photo, or with a dashed Add photo square. On the open Duo the camera keeps the left page while you type on the right.

Live viewfinder

The Orphaned tab opens on a live camera, docked above the tab bar where the thumb rests. The pile above it is a contact sheet: each envelope is its own print, a stacked print when it has several photos, and a manila blank when it has none. A shot opens a sheet for the cards and the note. Tap a print for its page.

Sizes and sources

  • Duo folded: 466 x 678 pt; the system tab bar starts at y 595 and is 83 pt tall with the bottom inset (measurement duo-fold-and-keyboard, 2026-11-08). The tab bar's capsule inside that band is drawn, not measured.
  • Duo open: 951 x 669 pt, fold strip 455.5 to 495.5 pt (same record). The tab bar is the Duo spine's pill under the right page: 64 pt tall, 24 pt in, 12 pt up (DuoBarMetrics, DuoSpine.swift).
  • Large phone: 402 pt wide, the window an 18 Pro UI test logged (TallyUITests, measured 2026-10-28). Its height, status bar and tab bar are not written anywhere I read. Drawn at 874 pt tall with the Duo's tab bar band: a placeholder, not a figure.
  • Status bar foot: unmeasured; drawn at 20 pt, the floor the Duo UI tests use. The keyboard is not drawn; on the folded Duo it covers 250 to 289 pt (same record).
  • Text sizes: Apple typography page, read 2026-11-21. Default (Large): Body 17, Footnote 13, Caption 1 12. AX5: Body 53, Footnote 44, Caption 1 43. Each native size is scaled by its style's ratio, as Font.custom(_:size:relativeTo:) does.
  • Photo tiles 56 pt, radius 8, manila while loading; the Lost tile (photo-thumbnails-and-viewer, PhotoStrip in src/app.jsx, PhotoViews.swift).
