Hand on every screen

Today's structure, with the gaps closed. Card in Hand opens on one page too, so the folded phone in one hand does the whole day. Its state outlives Close, a fold, a tab change and leaving the app. A count line says what today has done. A card no envelope holds goes to Orphaned with one press. Day's End shows the day and anything odd. The pile in your hand says how much is left; the app does not count it.

The pile

The bold one. Mail day becomes one run with a start, a middle and an end. You count the small piles first. A row of envelopes shows the pile and shrinks as you go. Each envelope ends with Next Envelope or Set Aside, so the count of what is left is always true. The last envelope lands a wax seal, and under it a short list of anything to come back to. Card in Hand still does the matching inside each envelope, as ruled.
