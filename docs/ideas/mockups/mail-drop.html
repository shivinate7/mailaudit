How it builds on what exists

  • The browser extension and mail drop feed one merge, matched on the marketplace order id. Whichever arrives first makes the line; the other fills gaps.
  • Each automatic change is its own log entry, with Undo.
  • A refund or cancel email proposes the existing "refunded" stamp. It never sets it.
  • A shipping email gives the lost-mail warning a real shipped date instead of the order date.
  • Never stored: the recipient name and address. The raw email goes after the keep window.

Owner decisions the plan lists (its recommendation in bold)

  1. Forwarding rule or read-only mailbox access? Forwarding; no mail login stored.
  2. Where does parsing run? The sync server, stripping personal fields before storing.
  3. Keep raw emails? 7 days, then delete.
  4. May a refund email set "refunded"? No; propose it.
  5. Extension and email disagree on a price? Newer source wins, disagreement shown in the log.
  6. The address leaks? A rotate button, and accept only the marketplace's sender domains.

Smallest first slice

  • Confirmation emails only: create or merge lines by order id, with the "from email" mark and an undoable log entry.
  • In this mockup that slice is states 1 to 5, plus the "4 cards added" log entry.
  • Open in the plan: whether the sync server can take inbound mail today is unknown.
