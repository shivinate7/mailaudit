    Owner decisions in the plan, and where each recommended answer shows

    1. Does a problem card count as received? Recommended: the package counts as arrived and the lost-mail warning stops; the card stays outstanding in Tally. See "Empty envelope".
    2. Photo required for a problem? Recommended: optional, with a visible "no photo" mark. See "Photo skipped".
    3. Outcome per card or per package? Recommended: per card, plus "Empty envelope" for the whole package. See "Problem chips".
    4. Does undo delete photos? Recommended: no. See "Undo".
    5. Blur the address label? Recommended: not in slice 1; warn whenever a photo leaves the app. Not drawn: no step in this plan sends a photo out of the app.
