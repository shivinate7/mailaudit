# A held card's box can sit under the keys

**What.** On Card in Hand on the open Duo, the Suspense and Released boxes sit below the matches. At large text sizes (AX3 in the mockup `duo-hand-notice-match`), or with three or more matches, both boxes sit under the keys, and nothing above the keys says a card is held or released. The owner then types the next card without seeing that one is waiting. Read from the mockup and the code; unmeasured on the simulator. The designer's open idea is a short "1 held" in the header. The gap closes when a held card shows above the keys at AX3 with three matches, and a test pins it.

**Status:** open.

**Decision:** `duo-card-in-hand`.
