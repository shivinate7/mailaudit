# A Suspense tap shifts the match list under the finger

**What.** On Card in Hand, tapping a match whose name two open envelopes share opens the Suspense box above New envelope and the match list. The tapped row moves down under the finger. No test pins it. A likely fix is to reserve the Suspense box's height, or to show it below the list. The gap closes when a tap on a match moves no row, and a test pins it.

**Status:** open.

**Decision:** `no-layout-shift-under-pointer`.
