5 · Fold to Seal wild

The phone is the envelope. Open it to open one envelope; close it to seal what you counted with a wax seal.

Central idea

The phone is the envelope: open it to open one, count the cards face up, close it to seal what arrived.

A mail-day session

  1. Pick up an envelope. Open the phone. It guesses the envelope; Wrong envelope picks another.
  2. The right page lays the expected cards face down.
  3. Tap each card in hand to turn it face up. Three of four Bolts shows one face down.
  4. Close the phone. The outer screen shows the wax seal and what is owed.
  5. A blank envelope: half-fold the phone. The hinge angle steps through senders.
  6. A late envelope in the queue carries Stamp for lost or refunded.

Better than a phone list

One envelope fills the whole screen, so nothing else can be ticked by mistake. Sealing is a clear end to each envelope, felt in the hand.

Biggest risk

Forty envelopes means forty folds, with cards in hand. Every pocket close would seal something. The hinge sensor and its angle reading are unmeasured.
