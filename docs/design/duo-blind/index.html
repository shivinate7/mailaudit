Mail day on the Duo · five concepts

Each concept differs at the root. All five use the same mail day: six sellers, Kestrel three of four Bolts in, one blank envelope. Tap a title for poses and notes. Hide removes one option.

1 · Card in Handpick

The card you hold is the search; the envelope is the answer.

2 · Account Book

Each seller is an account owing you cards; balance the book to zero.

3 · Sorting Frame

Find an envelope by thickness and age, in a wall of pigeonholes.

4 · The Letter

Today's mail written as a letter; answer by touching its words.

5 · Fold to Sealwild

The phone is the envelope; close it to seal what came.
