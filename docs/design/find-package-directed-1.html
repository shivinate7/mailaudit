Find a package by any word

Designer kind: directed. Direction: search-first. One box finds an order by any word. The results are ranked, and nothing else is on the screen.

Packages tab. iPhone Duo folded (466 x 678 pt), the folded Duo at AX5, the iPhone 18 Pro (402 x 874 pt), then the open Duo (951 x 669 pt, fold at 455.5 to 495.5 pt). Light theme only. Every frame works: type in the box, tap a result, tick a line. Notes: find-package-directed-1-notes.md.

Thumb Index

The best match sits right above the box, under the thumb. Worse matches stack upward. A tap opens the whole order. On the open Duo, the results take the left page and the order takes the right.

Deck

Each match is one whole order, ready to tick, on its own page. The best match comes first. Swipe for the next one. On the open Duo, two orders sit side by side, so two near-equal orders can be compared.

Due Now, the empty box ranks too

A sub-option for both concepts. With no words typed, the box ranks the orders most likely in today's mail: unreceived, ordered 5 to 14 days ago, oldest first. Hide it to keep the blank page.
