The "1 card" count

Menlo 11 regular is the iOS minimum text size. Apple's accessibility page: "Thicker weights are easier to read for smaller font sizes." A thin stroke at the minimum size is the likely place for a pixel sample to read lighter than inkSoft. Unmeasured.

The ready Save
