Overdue mail: a triage list

Designer kind: directed   Direction: triage list: a dedicated worklist ordered by days left, each row ends in a claim action

Two concepts. Both list every untracked, unreceived, unstamped order from day 14, ordered by the days left before day 30, and both end each row in the claim stamp. The Overdue desk is a page the masthead opens on any tab. The Claims tab is its own tab, with a claim sheet that holds the facts a claim needs. Tap rows, stamps, the masthead line, tabs and the calendar: they work.

Overdue desk

A new line under the masthead's missing line says how many orders may be lost and what they hold up. It opens the Overdue mail page, pushed on the tab you are on. Each row ends in a one-press Claim filed stamp, with Undo in the same slot. On the open Duo the list takes the left page and a claim-by calendar takes the right page.

Claims tab

A Claims tab between Orphaned and Ledger, with a badge of the orders listed. Each row carries a 30-day ruler. Its Claim button opens a sheet with the order number to copy, the cards still owed and the stamps. The masthead stays as it is today. On the open Duo the list takes the left page and the claim facts take the right page, as Tally and Orphaned spread.
