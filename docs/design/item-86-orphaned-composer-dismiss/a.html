Read from code: the 46 pt running head (RunningHead.height), the 16 pt gutters (OrphanedView.list), the form's parts and paints (ComposerView, OrphanedPaint), the tab order and badge (RootView).

The drag. HandScreen.swift puts .scrollDismissesKeyboard(.interactively) on its scroll. The same line on the Orphaned scroll lets the keys follow a downward drag and close. The green dashed line across the phone marks where the keys' top edge sat; the keys follow the finger once the drag passes it. The draft stays in the form, because model.draft(for:) holds it, not the field.
