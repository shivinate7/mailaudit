# Tab bar and Find on the open Duo: notes

Mockup: `chrome.html`. Deep links `#s=a` to `#s=e`. Open, flat, 951×669 pt, light theme only. Packages A is held constant.

1. **Option A: a custom bar under the left page.** Gain: nothing crosses the fold. Loss: it is not the system glass bar that native uses today (`phone-nav-is-bottom-bar`). Pick: A. Owner word: yes.
2. **Option B: the system bar, centred.** Gain: real iOS glass, no custom code. Loss: degen measured it at 29–71% of the width, so it crosses the fold. Pick: no. Owner word: yes.
3. **Option C: two half bars.** Gain: each thumb reaches two tabs. Loss: the tab order breaks at the fold. Pick: no. Owner word: yes.
4. **Option D: Find over the page that holds the list.** Gain: Find sits over what it filters. Loss: it moves between pages by view. Pick: D. Owner word: yes.
5. **Option E: Find at the top of the right page.** Gain: one fixed place. Loss: out of thumb reach, and unlike the phone. Pick: no. Owner word: yes.
6. **Tab order and icons stay as ruled.** Ledger, Orphaned, Tally, Packages, with the Orphaned count badge. Owner word: no.
