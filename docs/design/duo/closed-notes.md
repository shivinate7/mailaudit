# The closed outer screen: notes

Mockup: `closed.html`. Deep links `#s=a`, `#s=b`. Closed, 466×678 pt, light theme only. The state before the close is Packages A with Beta Games open.

1. **Option A: closing keeps the right page.** Gain: the card you were checking stays under your thumb. Loss: Back to the list is one more tap. Pick: A. Owner word: yes.
2. **Option B: closing keeps the left page.** Gain: you land where the phone app starts. Loss: you find the card again in the list. Pick: no. Owner word: yes.
3. **The outer screen is the phone layout.** It uses the phone's bottom bar and Find pill, as `phone-nav-is-bottom-bar` rules. Opening again restores both pages. Owner word: no.
