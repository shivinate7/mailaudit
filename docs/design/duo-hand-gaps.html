1 · The typed card's line option picked: accent bar

The October mockup marks the typed card's line with a green fill (its .line.now, index.html:101 and :163). In the build, that fill is already the done fill (PackageCard.token(.rowFill) gives greenSoft for a done line). So the October mark does not carry over as drawn. Two options.

2 · This envelope, with its cards so far carried over

Carried over from October poses 1 to 3 (index.html:153, :223, :256): under "This envelope", the cards checked in since the last New envelope, or "Nothing checked in yet". Kept to one line, so the row stays the 44 pt that New envelope's hit frame already sets, and the button never moves.

3 · Still owed here option picked: on the order line

The figure is the package's own "N left · $Y" (decision outstanding-value-per-level; the bundle's packagePill, which PackagesModel.pills already holds). October drew it under the lines with card names (index.html:167). With the build's keys spanning both pages from y 405, a line under three or more lines sits under the keys. So the place is drawn as two options.

4 · Keep held carried over

Carried over from October pose 2 (index.html:271 to :272): the offer carries Keep held beside Post. It goes in today's offer slot under the sender (decision duo-card-in-hand, the Post offer paragraph; item 42 still open). A press on Keep held leaves a receipt of the same height, as Post does.

5 · Held cards marked on their lines carried over

Carried over from October pose 2 (index.html:102, :267 to :268): a line whose card waits on the Suspense line gets a manila tint, and its detail ends "on the Suspense line". The tint and the words are added from HandScreen, so ItemLine, which Packages and Tally share, does not change.

6 · Post here at a tie carried over

Carried over from October pose 2 (index.html:228 to :243): each envelope of a tie lists its lines and has its own Post button. A tie still has no first choice: the buttons weigh the same, and nothing posts until you press one (orphaned-mail-never-decides). The button reads "Post N cards here", the act's name on the offer, not October's bare "Post here".

7 · The end of mail day carried over; what opens it: option picked, the empty query

Carried over from October pose 5 (index.html:171 to :195, :382 to :406): the left page lists today's envelopes, and the right page names only what is not normal. What opens it October does not say, since there the query was simply empty. Two options. The past-14-days box is today's transitBox, unchanged.

8 · Suspense ranks the accounts it fits carried over

Carried over from October pose 3 (index.html:286 to :312): an envelope with no sender, whose cards are each in two envelopes. Its cards wait on the Suspense line, and the right page ranks the accounts they fit by named copies. Post checks in the named cards only. The rank is the bundle's rankCandidates, the rule Orphaned already uses. When the top two rank equal, the page shows the tie of part 6 instead.
