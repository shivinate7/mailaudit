Keep both words, fix their roles in the glossary

"Held" names the card's state. "Suspense line" names the place it waits. Both stay on screen. The line says where the card waits, so the tint is never the only sign, and the line still says it when the box sits under the keys.

One word only: the line loses its words

The place word leaves the line. The held line keeps its manila tint and nothing else. The box keeps its name, so "Suspense" shows once, in the box, and "held" stays on the chip, the offer and Post.

One word only: the box loses its name

The other reading of the same option. The line keeps "on the Suspense line". The box drops its "Suspense" title and shows the card and its foot only.
